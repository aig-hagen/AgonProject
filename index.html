AgonProject

The platform to explore different approaches to formal argumentation.

AgonProject is a free, interactive tool for exploring formal argumentation. Build, visualize, and evaluate abstract argumentation frameworks and related formalisms, including bipolar, probabilistic, incomplete, and abstract dialectical frameworks, as well as argumentation with collective attacks. Model argumentation frameworks, compute their extensions and argument rankings under a wide range of argumentation semantics and export them to various formats, including LaTeX and SVG.
