reference: T22

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies Serialisability iff there are a [[selFn|selection function]] $\alpha$ and a [[termFn|termination function]] $\beta$ such that $\sigma(F) =$ [[serialisationSystem|$\mathcal{E}_{\alpha,\beta}(F)$]] for every [[AF]] $F$.
