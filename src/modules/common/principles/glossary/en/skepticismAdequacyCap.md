title: Skepticism Adequacy ($\preceq_\cap$)
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies $\preceq_\cap$-skepticism adequacy iff $F \preceq_A F'$ implies $\sigma(F) \preceq_\cap \sigma(F')$, where $F \preceq_A F'$ means both frameworks have the same conflicts and the attacks of $F'$ are a subset of those of $F$. Here $\mathcal{E}_1 \preceq_\cap \mathcal{E}_2$ iff $\bigcap \mathcal{E}_1 \subseteq \bigcap \mathcal{E}_2$.
