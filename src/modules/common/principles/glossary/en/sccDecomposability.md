title: SCC Decomposability
reference: BBCGTV14
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies SCC Decomposability iff its labellings on any framework are exactly the combinations of labellings that a local function assigns to each [[scc|SCC]], given the labelling of the SCC's attackers as input. Defined for labelling-based semantics.
