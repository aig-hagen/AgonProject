---
label: Allowing Abstention
title: Allowing Abstention
reference: BCG11
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies Allowing Abstention iff whenever an argument $a$ is in one [[extension]] $E_1 \in \sigma(F)$ and attacked by another $E_2 \in \sigma(F)$, there is an extension $E_3 \in \sigma(F)$ with $a \notin E_3 \cup E_3^+$.
