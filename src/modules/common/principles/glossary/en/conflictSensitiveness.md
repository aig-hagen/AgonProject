---
label: Conflict-Sensitiveness
title: Conflict-Sensitiveness
reference: DDLW15
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies Conflict-Sensitiveness iff for all [[extension|extensions]] $E_1, E_2 \in \sigma(F)$ with $E_1 \cup E_2 \notin \sigma(F)$, there are $a, b \in E_1 \cup E_2$ that never occur together in an extension.
