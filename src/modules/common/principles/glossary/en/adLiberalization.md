label: ad-liberalization
title: ad-Liberalization
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies ad-liberalization iff $\bigcup \mathit{ad}(F) \subseteq \bigcup \sigma(F)$ for every framework $F$, with a strict inclusion for some $F$: every credulously [[ADM|admissible]] argument is accepted, and sometimes more.
