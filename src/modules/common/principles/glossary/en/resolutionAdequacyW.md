label: resolution adequacy ($\preceq_W$)
title: Resolution Adequacy ($\preceq_W$)
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies $\preceq_W$-resolution adequacy iff $\mathit{UR}(F, \sigma) \preceq_W \sigma(F)$, where $\mathit{UR}(F, \sigma)$ collects the extensions of all resolutions of $F$ (the frameworks obtained by turning each symmetric attack into a single direction). Here $\mathcal{E}_1 \preceq_W \mathcal{E}_2$ iff every $E_2 \in \mathcal{E}_2$ has some $E_1 \in \mathcal{E}_1$ with $E_1 \subseteq E_2$.
