title: Resolution Adequacy ($\preceq_S$)
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies $\preceq_S$-resolution adequacy iff $\mathit{UR}(F, \sigma) \preceq_S \sigma(F)$, where $\mathit{UR}(F, \sigma)$ collects the extensions of all resolutions of $F$ (the frameworks obtained by turning each symmetric attack into a single direction). Here $\mathcal{E}_1 \preceq_S \mathcal{E}_2$ iff $\mathcal{E}_1 \preceq_W \mathcal{E}_2$ and every $E_1 \in \mathcal{E}_1$ has some $E_2 \in \mathcal{E}_2$ with $E_1 \subseteq E_2$.
