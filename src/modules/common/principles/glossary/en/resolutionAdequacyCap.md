label: resolution adequacy ($\preceq_\cap$)
title: Resolution Adequacy ($\preceq_\cap$)
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies $\preceq_\cap$-resolution adequacy iff $\mathit{UR}(F, \sigma) \preceq_\cap \sigma(F)$, where $\mathit{UR}(F, \sigma)$ collects the extensions of all resolutions of $F$ (the frameworks obtained by turning each symmetric attack into a single direction). Here $\mathcal{E}_1 \preceq_\cap \mathcal{E}_2$ iff $\bigcap \mathcal{E}_1 \subseteq \bigcap \mathcal{E}_2$.
