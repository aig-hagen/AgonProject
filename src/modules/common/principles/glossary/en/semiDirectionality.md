label: Semi-Directionality
title: Semi-Directionality
reference: TV18
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies Semi-Directionality iff for every framework $F$ and every [[unattackedSet|unattacked set]] $U$, $\sigma(F|_U) \subseteq \{E \cap U \mid E \in \sigma(F)\}$ (the other half of [[directionality]]).
