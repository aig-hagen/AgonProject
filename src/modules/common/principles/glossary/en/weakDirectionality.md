---
label: Weak Directionality
title: Weak Directionality
reference: TV18
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies Weak Directionality iff for every framework $F$ and every [[unattackedSet|unattacked set]] $U$, $\sigma(F|_U) \supseteq \{E \cap U \mid E \in \sigma(F)\}$ (one half of [[directionality]]).
