---
label: Strong Admissibility
title: Strong Admissibility
reference: BG07
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies Strong Admissibility iff for every [[extension]] $E \in \sigma(F)$, $E$ strongly defends each $a \in E$: every attacker of $a$ is attacked by some $c \in E \setminus \{a\}$ that is itself strongly defended by $E \setminus \{a\}$ (see [[SAD]]).
