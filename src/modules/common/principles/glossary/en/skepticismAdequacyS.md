label: skepticism adequacy ($\preceq_S$)
title: Skepticism Adequacy ($\preceq_S$)
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies $\preceq_S$-skepticism adequacy iff $F \preceq_A F'$ implies $\sigma(F) \preceq_S \sigma(F')$, where $F \preceq_A F'$ means both frameworks have the same conflicts and the attacks of $F'$ are a subset of those of $F$. Here $\mathcal{E}_1 \preceq_S \mathcal{E}_2$ iff $\mathcal{E}_1 \preceq_W \mathcal{E}_2$ and every $E_1 \in \mathcal{E}_1$ has some $E_2 \in \mathcal{E}_2$ with $E_1 \subseteq E_2$.
