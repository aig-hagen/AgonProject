---
label: splitting
title: Splitting
---

A [[semantics]] $\sigma$ satisfies splitting iff for every splitting $(F_1, F_2, R_3)$ of $F$ (no attacks from $F_2$ into $F_1$), the extensions of $F$ are exactly the sets $E_1 \cup E_2$ with $E_1 \in \sigma(F_1)$ and $E_2$ an extension of $F_2$ after removing what $E_1$ attacks.
