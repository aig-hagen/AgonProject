---
label: $\textsc{Skept}_\sigma$
title: Skeptical Acceptance $\textsc{Skept}_\sigma$
short: Skeptical Acceptance
reference: DD18
---

Given an [[AF]] $F$ and an argument $a$, decide whether $a$ is [[skepticalAcceptance|skeptically accepted]], i.e. contained in every [[extension|$\sigma$-extension]] of $F$. If $F$ has no $\sigma$-extension, every argument counts as skeptically accepted.
