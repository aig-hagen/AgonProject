---
label: $\Sigma_2^{\mathsf{P}}$
title: Complexity Class $\Sigma_2^{\mathsf{P}}$
short: $\Sigma_2^{\mathsf{P}}$
reference: S76
---

The decision problems of the form $\exists y_1 \forall y_2\, W(x, y_1, y_2)$, where $y_1, y_2$ have size polynomial in $|x|$ and $W$ is decidable in polynomial time. Equivalently, the problems solvable in [[classNP|$\mathsf{NP}$]] with an $\mathsf{NP}$ oracle, $\Sigma_2^{\mathsf{P}} = \mathsf{NP}^{\mathsf{NP}}$. It forms the second level of the polynomial hierarchy together with [[classPi2P|$\Pi_2^{\mathsf{P}}$]].
