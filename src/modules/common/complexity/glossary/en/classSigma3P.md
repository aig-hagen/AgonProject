---
label: $\Sigma_3^{\mathsf{P}}$
title: Complexity Class $\Sigma_3^{\mathsf{P}}$
short: $\Sigma_3^{\mathsf{P}}$
reference: S76
---

The decision problems of the form $\exists y_1 \forall y_2 \exists y_3\, W(x, y_1, y_2, y_3)$, where the $y_i$ have size polynomial in $|x|$ and $W$ is decidable in polynomial time. Equivalently, the problems solvable in [[classNP|$\mathsf{NP}$]] with a [[classSigma2P|$\Sigma_2^{\mathsf{P}}$]] oracle.
