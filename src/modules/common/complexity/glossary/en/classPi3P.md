---
label: $\Pi_3^{\mathsf{P}}$
title: Complexity Class $\Pi_3^{\mathsf{P}}$
short: $\Pi_3^{\mathsf{P}}$
reference: S76
---

The decision problems of the form $\forall y_1 \exists y_2 \forall y_3\, W(x, y_1, y_2, y_3)$, where the $y_i$ have size polynomial in $|x|$ and $W$ is decidable in polynomial time. Equivalently, the problems solvable in [[classCoNP|$\mathsf{coNP}$]] with a [[classSigma2P|$\Sigma_2^{\mathsf{P}}$]] oracle. These are the complements of the problems in [[classSigma3P|$\Sigma_3^{\mathsf{P}}$]].
