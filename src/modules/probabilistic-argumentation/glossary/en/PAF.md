---
label: PAF
title: Probabilistic Argumentation Framework (PAF)
---

A probabilistic argumentation framework is a tuple $(A, R, P)$ such that $(A, R)$ is an [[AF]] and $P : A \cup R \to (0,1]$ assigns each [[argumentProbability|argument]] and [[attackProbability|attack]] the probability that it is present. We consider semantics under  the [[constellations]].
