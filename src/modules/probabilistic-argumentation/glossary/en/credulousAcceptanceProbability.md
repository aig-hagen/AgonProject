---
label: credulous acceptance probability
title: Credulous Acceptance Probability
symbol: P_{\sigma,c}(a)
reference: H12
---

Given a [[semantics]] $\sigma$, the probability that an argument $a \in A$ of a [[PAF]] is [[credulousAcceptance|credulously accepted]] is the sum of the [[subframeworkProbability|probabilities]] of all [[subframework|subframeworks]] $F'$ in which $a$ is credulously accepted: $P_{\sigma,c}(a) = \sum_{F' : a \in \bigcup \sigma(F')} P(F')$.
