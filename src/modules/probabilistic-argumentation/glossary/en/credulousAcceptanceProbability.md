label: credulous acceptance probability
title: Credulous Acceptance Probability
reference: H12
---

The probability that $a$ is [[credulousAcceptance|credulously accepted]] under $\sigma$ is the sum of the [[subframeworkProbability|probabilities]] of all subframeworks $F'$ in which $a$ is credulously accepted: $P_\sigma(a) = \sum_{F' : a \in \bigcup \sigma(F')} P(F')$.
