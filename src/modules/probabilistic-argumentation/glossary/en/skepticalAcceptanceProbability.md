---
label: skeptical acceptance probability
title: Skeptical Acceptance Probability
symbol: P_{\sigma,s}(a)
reference: H12
---

Given a [[semantics]] $\sigma$, the probability that an argument $a \in A$ of a [[PAF]] is [[skepticalAcceptance|skeptically accepted]] is the sum of the [[subframeworkProbability|probabilities]] of all [[subframework|subframeworks]] $F'$ in which $a$ is skeptically accepted: $P_{\sigma,s}(a) = \sum_{F' : a \in \bigcap \sigma(F')} P(F')$.
