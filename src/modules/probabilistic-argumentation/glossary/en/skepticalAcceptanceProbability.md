---
label: skeptical acceptance probability
title: Skeptical Acceptance Probability
---

The probability that $a$ is [[skepticalAcceptance|skeptically accepted]] under $\sigma$ is the sum of the [[subframeworkProbability|probabilities]] of all subframeworks $F'$ in which $a$ is skeptically accepted: $P_\sigma(a) = \sum_{F' : a \in \bigcap \sigma(F')} P(F')$.
