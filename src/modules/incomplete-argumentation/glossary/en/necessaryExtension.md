title: Necessary Extension
reference: BNRS18
---

Given a [[semantics]] $\sigma$, a set $E \subseteq A$ is a necessary [[extension|$\sigma$-extension]] of an [[IAF]] $I = (A, A^?, R, R^?)$ iff $E \in \sigma(F^*)$ for every [[completion]] $F^*$.

## Remarks

This is the necessary verification of [@BNRS18] for definite sets; [@BNRS18] also admits sets $S \subseteq A \cup A^?$ and then asks whether $S \cap A^*$ is a $\sigma$-extension of every completion.
