---
label: strongly admissible
title: Strong Admissibility (SetAF)
short: Strong Admissibility
symbol: \mathsf{sad}
reference: BBR25
---

A set $E \subseteq A$ is strongly admissible iff $E$ is conflict-free and every $a \in E$ is defended by some strongly admissible $E' \subseteq E \setminus \{a\}$.
