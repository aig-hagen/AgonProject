---
label: weak defence
title: Weak Defence (SetAF)
reference: BKU24
---

A set of arguments $E$ weakly defends $X \subseteq A$ if for each $(T, h) \in R$ with $h \in X$, one of the following two conditions holds: $E$ attacks $T$, or simultaneously (1) there is no $E' \in \mathsf{wad}($[[setafReduct|$S^E$]]) with $T \subseteq E \cup E'$, and (2) there is some $X'$ such that $X \subseteq X' \in$ [[setafWAD|$\mathsf{wad}(S)$]].
