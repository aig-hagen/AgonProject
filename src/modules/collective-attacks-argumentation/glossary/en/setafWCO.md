---
label: weakly complete
title: Weakly Complete Semantics (SetAF)
short: Weakly Complete Semantics
symbol: \mathsf{wco}
reference: BKU24
---

A set $E \subseteq A$ is a weakly complete [[extension]] iff $E$ is [[setafWAD|weakly admissible]] and $E' \subseteq E$ for every $E' \supseteq E$ that $E$ [[setafWeakDefence|weakly defends]].
