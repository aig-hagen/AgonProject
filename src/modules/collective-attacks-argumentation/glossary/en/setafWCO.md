---
label: weakly complete
title: Weakly Complete Semantics (SetAF)
short: Weakly Complete Semantics
reference: BKU24
---

A set $E \in$ [[setafWAD|$\mathsf{wad}(S)$]] is weakly complete ($E \in \mathsf{wco}(S)$) iff for each $X \supseteq E$ such that $E$ [[setafWeakDefence|weakly defends]] $X$, we have $E = X$.
