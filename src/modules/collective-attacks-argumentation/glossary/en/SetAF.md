label: SetAF
title: Argumentation Framework with Collective Attacks (SetAF)
reference: NP06
---

An argumentation framework with collective attacks (SetAF) $S = (A, R)$ consists of a finite set of arguments $A$ and an attack relation $R \subseteq (2^A \setminus \{\emptyset\}) \times A$.
