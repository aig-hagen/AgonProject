---
label: weakly admissible
title: Weak Admissibility (SetAF)
reference: BKU24
---

A set of arguments $E \subseteq A$ is weakly admissible in $S$ ($E \in \mathsf{wad}(S)$) iff (1) $E \in \mathsf{cf}(S)$, and (2) for each $(T, h) \in R$ with $h \in E$ and $T \cap$ [[setafRange|$E^+_R$]] $= \emptyset$ there is no $E' \in \mathsf{wad}($[[setafReduct|$S^E$]]) such that $T \cap A' \subseteq E'$.
