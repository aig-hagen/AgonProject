---
label: weakly admissible
title: Weak Admissibility (SetAF)
short: Weak Admissibility
symbol: \mathsf{wad}
reference: BKU24
---

A set $E \subseteq A$ is weakly admissible iff $E$ is conflict-free and $T \cap A' \not\subseteq E'$ for every $E' \in \mathsf{wad}($[[setafReduct|$S^E$]]$)$ and every $(T, h) \in R$ with $h \in E$ and $T \cap$ [[setafRange|$E^+_R$]] $= \emptyset$, where $S^E = (A', R')$.
