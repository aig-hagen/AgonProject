---
label: consensus
title: Consensus Operator
symbol: \sqcap
reference: BESWW13
---

The consensus $v_1 \sqcap v_2$ of two [[threeValuedInterpretation|three-valued interpretations]] $v_1, v_2$ maps each $a \in A$ to $\mathsf{t}$ if $v_1(a) = v_2(a) = \mathsf{t}$, to $\mathsf{f}$ if $v_1(a) = v_2(a) = \mathsf{f}$, and to $\mathsf{u}$ otherwise.
