---
label: consensus
title: Consensus Operator
reference: BESWW18
---

The consensus operator $\sqcap$ combines two [[threeValuedInterpretation|three-valued interpretations]] $v_1$ and $v_2$ into a new interpretation $v_3 = v_1 \sqcap v_2$ such that for each $a \in A$: $v_3(a) = \mathbf{t}$ iff $v_1(a) = v_2(a) = \mathbf{t}$, $v_3(a) = \mathbf{f}$ iff $v_1(a) = v_2(a) = \mathbf{f}$, and $v_3(a) = \mathbf{u}$ otherwise.
