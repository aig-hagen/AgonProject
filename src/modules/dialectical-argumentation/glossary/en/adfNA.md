---
label: naive
title: Naive Semantics (ADF)
short: Naive Semantics
symbol: \mathsf{na}
reference: SW15
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is a naive model iff $v$ is [[adfCF|conflict-free]] and there is no conflict-free model $v'$ such that $v <_i v'$ in the [[informationOrdering]].
