---
label: conflict-free
title: Conflict-Freeness (ADF)
short: Conflict-Freeness
reference: SW15
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is a conflict-free model iff, for every argument $a \in A$, $v(a) = \mathbf{t}$ implies that $C_a$ is satisfied by at least one [[completions|two-valued completion]] of $v$, and $v(a) = \mathbf{f}$ implies that $C_a$ is falsified by at least one two-valued completion of $v$.
