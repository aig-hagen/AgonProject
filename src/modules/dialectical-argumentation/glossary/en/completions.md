---
label: completions
title: Two-Valued Completions
symbol: [v]_2
reference: BESWW13
---

The two-valued completions of a [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] are $[v]_2 = \{ w \mid v \leq_i w \text{ and } w^{-1}(\mathsf{u}) = \emptyset \}$, where $\leq_i$ is the [[informationOrdering]].
