---
label: semi-two-valued
title: Semi-Two-Valued Semantics (ADF)
short: Semi-Two-Valued Semantics
symbol: \mathsf{s2v}
reference: KVV21
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is a semi-two-valued model iff $v$ is [[adfCO|complete]] and $v^{-1}(\mathsf{u})$ is $\subseteq$-minimal among all $w^{-1}(\mathsf{u})$ such that $w$ is a complete model.
