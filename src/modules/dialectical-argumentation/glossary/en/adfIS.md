---
label: initial models
title: Initial Models (ADF)
short: Initial Models
symbol: \mathsf{is}
reference: BT25
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is an initial model iff $v$ is [[adfADM|admissible]] with $v \neq v_\mathsf{u}$ and there is no admissible model $v' \neq v_\mathsf{u}$ with $v' <_i v$ in the [[informationOrdering]], where $v_\mathsf{u}$ maps every argument to $\mathsf{u}$.
