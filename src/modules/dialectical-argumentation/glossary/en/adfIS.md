---
label: initial models
title: Initial Models (ADF)
short: Initial Models
reference: BT25
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is an initial model iff $v$ is [[adfADM|admissible]] with $v \neq v_\mathbf{u}$ and there is no admissible model $v' \neq v_\mathbf{u}$ with $v' <_i v$ in the [[informationOrdering]], where $v_\mathbf{u}$ maps every argument to $\mathbf{u}$.
