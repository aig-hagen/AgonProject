label: acceptance condition
title: Acceptance Condition
symbol: C_a
reference: BW10
---

The acceptance condition $C_a$ of an argument $a \in A$ in an [[ADF]] $D = (A, L, C)$ is modelled as a propositional formula over [[parents|$par(a)$]] that determines when $a$ is accepted, given the set of accepted parents.
