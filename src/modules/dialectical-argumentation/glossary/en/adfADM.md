---
label: admissible
title: Admissibility (ADF)
short: Admissibility
symbol: \mathsf{ad}
reference: BESWW13
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] $D$ is an admissible model iff $v \leq_i \Gamma_D(v)$, where $\leq_i$ is the [[informationOrdering]] and $\Gamma_D$ the [[operator|characteristic operator]].
