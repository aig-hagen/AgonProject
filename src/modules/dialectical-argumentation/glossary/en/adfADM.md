label: admissible
title: Admissibility (ADF)
short: Admissibility
reference: BESWW13
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is an admissible model iff $v \leq_i \Gamma_D(v)$ in the [[informationOrdering]], where $\Gamma_D(v)$ is the [[operator|characteristic operator]] for ADFs.
