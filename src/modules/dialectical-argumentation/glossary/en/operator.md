---
label: characteristic operator
title: Characteristic Operator
---

The characteristic operator $\Gamma_D$ of an [[ADF]] $D$ is defined as $\Gamma_D(v)(a) = \sqcap \{ v'(C_a) \mid v' \in [v]_2 \}$ for all $a \in A$. It maps $v$ to the [[consensus|consensus]] over its [[completions|two-valued completions]].
