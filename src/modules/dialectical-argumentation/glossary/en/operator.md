---
label: characteristic operator
title: Characteristic Operator
symbol: \Gamma_D
reference: BESWW13
---

The characteristic operator $\Gamma_D$ of an [[ADF]] $D$ maps a [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ to $\Gamma_D(v)$ with $\Gamma_D(v)(a) = \sqcap \{ w(C_a) \mid w \in [v]_2 \}$ for all $a \in A$, where $[v]_2$ are the [[completions]] of $v$ and $\sqcap$ is the [[consensus]].
