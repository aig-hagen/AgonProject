---
label: semi-stable
title: Semi-Stable Semantics (ADF)
short: Semi-Stable Semantics
symbol: \mathsf{sst}
reference: KVV21
---

A [[adfS2V|semi-two-valued model]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] $D$ is a semi-stable model iff $v^{-1}(\mathsf{t}) = w^{-1}(\mathsf{t})$, where $w$ is the [[adfGR|grounded model]] of the [[subReduct|sub-reduct]] $D^v$.
