---
label: stable
title: Stable Semantics (ADF)
---

A two-valued interpretation $v$ of an [[ADF]] is a stable model iff $v$ is a [[adfMOD2|two-valued model]] of $D$ and $v^{-1}(\mathbf{t}) = v_{gr}^{-1}(\mathbf{t})$, where $v_{gr}$ is the [[adfGR|grounded model]] of the [[reducedAdf|reduced ADF]] $D_{\downarrow v}$.
