---
label: stable
title: Stable Semantics (ADF)
short: Stable Semantics
symbol: \mathsf{st}
reference: BESWW13
---

A [[twoValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] $D$ is a stable model iff $v$ is a [[adfMOD2|two-valued model]] of $D$ and $v^{-1}(\mathsf{t}) = w^{-1}(\mathsf{t})$, where $w$ is the [[adfGR|grounded model]] of the [[reducedAdf|reduced ADF]] $D_{\downarrow v}$.
