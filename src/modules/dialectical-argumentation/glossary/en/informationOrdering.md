title: Information Ordering
reference: BESWW13
---

The information ordering $\leq_i$ on truth values is given by $\mathbf{u} <_i \mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{u} <_i \mathbf{f}$. For two [[threeValuedInterpretation|three-valued interpretations]] $v_1, v_2$ we have $v_1 \leq_i v_2$ iff $v_1(a) \leq_i v_2(a)$ for all $a \in A$.
