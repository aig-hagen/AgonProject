label: two-valued interpretation
title: Two-Valued Interpretation
reference: BESWW13
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is two-valued iff $v(a) \in \{\mathbf{t}, \mathbf{f}\}$ for all $a \in A$.

## Properties

Among all three-valued interpretations, the two-valued ones are the maximal elements with respect to the [[informationOrdering]] $\leq_i$; those that extend a three-valued interpretation $v$ are its [[completions]] $[v]_2$ [@BESWW13].
