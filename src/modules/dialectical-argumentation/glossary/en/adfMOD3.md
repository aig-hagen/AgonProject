---
label: three-valued model
title: Three-Valued Model (ADF)
reference: BESWW13
---

A [[threeValuedInterpretation]] $v$ of an [[ADF]] is a three-valued model iff $v(a) \neq \mathbf{u}$ implies $v(a) = v(C_a)$ for all $a \in A$, where $v(C_a)$ is evaluated in Kleene's strong three-valued logic: negation switches $\mathbf{t}$ and $\mathbf{f}$ and leaves $\mathbf{u}$ unchanged, a conjunction is $\mathbf{t}$ if both conjuncts are $\mathbf{t}$, $\mathbf{f}$ if some conjunct is $\mathbf{f}$, and $\mathbf{u}$ otherwise; disjunction is dual.
