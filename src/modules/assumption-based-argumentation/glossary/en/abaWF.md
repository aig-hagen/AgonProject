---
label: well-founded
title: Well-Founded Semantics (ABA)
short: Well-Founded Semantics
symbol: \mathsf{wf}
reference: CFST18
---

A set of assumptions $S \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ is well-founded iff $S$ is the intersection of all [[abaCO|complete]] extensions. For [[abaFlat]] ABA frameworks, it coincides with the [[abaGR|grounded]] extension.
