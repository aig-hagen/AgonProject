---
label: attack
title: Attack (ABA)
short: Attack
reference: CFST18
---

A set $S \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ attacks a set $T \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ iff there are $S' \subseteq S$ and $a \in T$ such that [[abaDerivation|$S' \vdash \overline{a}$]] for the [[abaContrary|contrary]] $\overline{a}$ of $a$.
