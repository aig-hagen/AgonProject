---
label: $S_D$
title: Instantiated SetAF
symbol: S_D
reference: KRU22
---

For a [[abaFlat|flat]] [[ABAF]] $D = (\mathcal{L}, \mathcal{R}, \mathcal{A}, \overline{\phantom{a}})$, the corresponding [[SetAF]] is $S_D = (\mathcal{A}, R)$ with $R = \{(T, h) \mid h \in \mathcal{A},\ T \subseteq \mathcal{A},\ T \vdash \overline{h}\}$, where $\vdash$ is [[abaDerivation|tree-derivability]].
