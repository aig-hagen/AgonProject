---
label: admissible
title: Admissibility (ABA)
short: Admissibility
symbol: \mathsf{ad}
reference: CFST18
---

A set of assumptions $S \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ is admissible iff $S$ is [[abaClosure|closed]], [[abaCF]] and [[abaAttack|attacks]] every closed $T \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ that attacks $S$.
