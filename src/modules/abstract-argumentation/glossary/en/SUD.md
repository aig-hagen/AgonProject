---
label: strongly undisputed
title: Strongly Undisputed Sets
symbol: \mathsf{sud}
reference: T23
---

A set $E \subseteq A$ is strongly undisputed iff $E$ is [[CF|conflict-free]] and the [[reduct]] $F^E$ has no non-empty [[UD|undisputed]] set.

## Remarks

That is, $\mathsf{sud} = \mathsf{cf}^{\mathsf{ud}}$, a [[VR|vacuous reduct semantics]].
