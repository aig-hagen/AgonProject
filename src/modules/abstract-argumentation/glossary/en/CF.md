---
label: conflict-free
title: Conflict-Freeness
symbol: \mathsf{cf}
reference: D95
---

A set $E \subseteq A$ is conflict-free iff there are no $a,b \in E$ with $(a,b) \in R$.

## Signature

A set $\mathbb{S}$ of sets of arguments is in [[signature|$\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{cf})$]] iff $\mathbb{S} \neq \emptyset$, $\mathbb{S}$ is downward-closed and tight [@DDLW15].
