---
label: counting
title: Counting Ranking
symbol: \theta^{\mathsf{Cnt}}
reference: PLZL15
---

The counting ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ and damping factor $\alpha \in (0,1)$ a [[rankingSemantics|ranking]] $\succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{Cnt}}}_F$ on $A$ that counts the attack paths ending in each argument, weighted by $(\alpha/N)^\ell$ for length $\ell$, with $N = \|A\|_\infty$ the normalisation factor: defenders (even $\ell$) count positively, attackers (odd $\ell$) negatively.
