title: Serialisation Graph
reference: BT26

The $\alpha\beta$-serialisation of an [[AF]] $F$ is the graph of all [[serialisationState|serialisation states]] reachable from $(F, \emptyset)$ in the [[serialisationSystem|serialisation system]] of a [[selFn|selection function]] $\alpha$, with an edge labelled $S$ for each transition that adds the [[IS|initial set]] $S$. The states accepted by a [[termFn|termination function]] $\beta$ are highlighted; they form [[serialisationSystem|$\mathcal{E}_{\alpha,\beta}(F)$]].
