A set $E \subseteq A$ is a naive [[extension]] iff $E$ is a $\subseteq$-maximal [[CF|conflict-free]] set.

## Signature

A set $\mathbb{S}$ of sets of arguments is in [[signature|$\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{na})$]] iff $\mathbb{S} \neq \emptyset$, $\mathbb{S}$ is incomparable and $\mathrm{dcl}_\mathbb{S}$ is tight [@DDLW15].
