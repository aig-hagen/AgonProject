---
label: categorizer function
title: Categorizer Function
symbol: \mathsf{Cat}_F
reference: BH01
---

The categorizer function $\mathsf{Cat}_F \colon A \to (0,1]$ is the unique function with $\mathsf{Cat}_F(a) = 1$ if [[attackersSet|$a^-$]] $= \emptyset$ and $\mathsf{Cat}_F(a) = \frac{1}{1 + \sum_{b \in a^-} \mathsf{Cat}_F(b)}$ otherwise.
