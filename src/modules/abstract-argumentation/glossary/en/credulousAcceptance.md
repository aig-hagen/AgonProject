title: Credulous Acceptance
reference: DD18
---

Given a [[semantics]] $\sigma$, an argument $a \in A$ is credulously accepted iff $a \in E$ for some [[extension|$\sigma$-extension]] $E \in \sigma(F)$, i.e. $a \in \mathsf{cred}_\sigma(F) = \bigcup \sigma(F)$.

## Remarks

The associated decision problem is [[problemCred]].
