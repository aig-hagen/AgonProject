A set $E \subseteq A$ is the grounded [[extension]] iff $E$ is a $\subseteq$-minimal [[CO|complete]] extension. It is the least fixed point of the [[charFunction|characteristic function]] $\Delta_F$.

## Signature

A set $\mathbb{S}$ of sets of arguments is in [[signature|$\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{gr})$]] iff $\lvert\mathbb{S}\rvert = 1$ [@DDLW15].
