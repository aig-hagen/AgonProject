A set $E \subseteq A$ is strongly admissible iff every $a \in E$ is [[defends|defended]] by some strongly admissible $E' \subseteq E \setminus \{a\}$.

## Signature

A set $\mathbb{S}$ of sets of arguments is in [[signature|$\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{sad})$]] iff $\mathbb{S}$ is decomposable [@D20].
