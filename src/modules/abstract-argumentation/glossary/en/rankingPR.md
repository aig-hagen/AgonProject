The propagation ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ a [[rankingSemantics|ranking]] $\succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{Propa}}}_F$ on $A$ by giving each argument an initial value, propagating it along attack paths, and comparing the resulting vectors, in one of three variants $\mathsf{Propa}_\epsilon$, $\mathsf{Propa}_{1+\epsilon}$, $\mathsf{Propa}_{1\to\epsilon}$.

## Remarks

AgonProject computes this ranking for acyclic AFs only.
