---
label: burden-based
title: Burden-Based Ranking
---

The burden-based ranking associates to any AF $F$ a ranking $\succeq_F^\mathrm{BB}$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A: a \succeq_F^\mathrm{BB} b$ iff $\mathrm{Bur}(b) \succeq_{lex} \mathrm{Bur}(a)$, where $\mathrm{Bur}$ is the [[BurdenNumber]].
