---
label: burden-based
title: Burden-Based Ranking
symbol: \theta^{\mathsf{Bbs}}
reference: AB13
---

The burden-based ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ a [[rankingSemantics|ranking]] $\succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{Bbs}}}_F$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A$: $a \succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{Bbs}}}_F b$ iff $\mathsf{Bur}_F(a) \preceq_{\mathrm{lex}} \mathsf{Bur}_F(b)$, where $\mathsf{Bur}_F$ is the [[BurdenNumber|burden number]].
