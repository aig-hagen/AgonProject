The [[termFn|termination function]] $\beta_{\mathsf{uc}}$ is defined by $\beta_{\mathsf{uc}}(F,E) = 1$ iff [[unattackedInitial|$\mathsf{is}^{\not\leftarrow}(F)$]] $\cup$ [[unchallengedInitial|$\mathsf{is}^{\not\leftrightarrow}(F)$]] $= \emptyset$.
