---
label: weak defence
title: Weak Defence
reference: BBU20
---

A set $E \subseteq A$ weakly defends $E' \subseteq A$ iff for every $b \in$ [[attackersSet|$E'^-$]], either $E$ attacks $b$, or $b \notin E$, $b \notin \bigcup \mathsf{wad}($[[reduct|$F^E$]]$)$, and $E' \subseteq E''$ for some [[WAD|weakly admissible]] $E''$.
