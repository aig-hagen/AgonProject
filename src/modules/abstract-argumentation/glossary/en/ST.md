A set $E \subseteq A$ is a stable [[extension]] iff $E$ is [[CF|conflict-free]] and [[range|$E^\oplus$]] $= A$.

## Signature

A set $\mathbb{S}$ of sets of arguments is in [[signature|$\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{st})$]] iff $\mathbb{S}$ is incomparable and tight [@DDLW15].
