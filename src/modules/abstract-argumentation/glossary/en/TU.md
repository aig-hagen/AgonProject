The tuples ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ a [[rankingSemantics|ranking]] $\succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{Tu}}}_F$ on $A$ that compares arguments by the lengths of the defence (even) and attack (odd) branches ending in them; two arguments can be incomparable.

## Remarks

AgonProject computes this ranking for acyclic AFs only.
