title: Argument-ranking Semantics
reference: AB13

An argument-ranking semantics $\theta$ assigns to each [[AF]] $F$ a [[preorder]] $\succeq^\theta_F$ on $A$, the ranking of $F$; $a \succeq^\theta_F b$ means that $a$ is at least as acceptable as $b$.
