---
label: $mn$-complete
title: $mn$-Complete Semantics
reference: GM15
---

For integers $m,n \geq 1$, a set $E \subseteq A$ is an $mn$-complete [[extension]] iff $E \subseteq$ [[gradedNeutralityFunction|$n_m(E)$]] and $E =$ [[gradedDefenseFunction|$d_n^m(E)$]], i.e. $E$ is $m$-conflict-free and a fixed point of graded defence.
