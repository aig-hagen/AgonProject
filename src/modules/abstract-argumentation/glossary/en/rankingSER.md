---
label: serialisability-based
title: Serialisability-based Ranking
reference: BT22b
---

The Serialisability-based ranking associates to any AF $F$ a ranking $\succeq_F^\mathrm{Ser}$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A: a \succeq_F^\mathrm{Ser} b$ iff $\mathrm{ser}_F(a) \leq \mathrm{ser}_F(b)$, where $\mathrm{ser}_F(a)$ is the [[serialisationIndex]].
