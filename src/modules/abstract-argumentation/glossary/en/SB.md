---
label: strategy-based
title: Strategy-Based Ranking
---

The strategy-based ranking associates to any AF $F$ a ranking $\succeq_F^\mathrm{SB}$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A: a \succeq_F^\mathrm{SB} b$ iff $\mathrm{s}(a) \geq \mathrm{s}(b)$ where $\mathrm{s}(a)$ is the strength of $a$ based on a [[strategyGame|two-person zero-sum game]].
