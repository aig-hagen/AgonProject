label: strategy-based
title: Strategy-Based Ranking
symbol: \theta^{\mathsf{SB}}
reference: MT08
---

The strategy-based ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ a [[rankingSemantics|ranking]] $\succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{SB}}}_F$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A$: $a \succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{SB}}}_F b$ iff $\mathsf{s}_F(a) \geq \mathsf{s}_F(b)$, where $\mathsf{s}_F(a)$ is the value of the [[strategyGame|strategic game]] for $a$.
