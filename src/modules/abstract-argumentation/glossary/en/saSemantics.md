label: SA-semantics
title: Social Argumentation Semantics
reference: LM11
---

A social argumentation semantics is defined by the tuple $\langle L, \tau, \curlywedge, \curlyvee, \neg \rangle$, where $L$ is a totally ordered set of labels, $\tau$ is an attenuation factor, $\curlywedge$ is a T-norm, $\curlyvee$ is a T-CoNorm and $\neg$ is a negation operator.
