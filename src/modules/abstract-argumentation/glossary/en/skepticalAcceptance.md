title: Skeptical Acceptance
reference: DD18
---

Given a [[semantics]] $\sigma$, an argument $a \in A$ is skeptically accepted iff $a \in E$ for every [[extension|$\sigma$-extension]] $E \in \sigma(F)$, i.e. $a \in \mathsf{skep}_\sigma(F) = \bigcap \sigma(F)$.

## Remarks

If $\sigma(F) = \emptyset$, every argument is skeptically accepted [@DD18].

The associated decision problem is [[problemSkept]].
