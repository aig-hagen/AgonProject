label: strategic game
title: Two-Person Zero-Sum Strategic Game
---

For an [[AF]] $F=(A,R)$ and an argument $a \in A$, the associated two-person zero-sum game lets the proponent choose a set containing $a$ and the opponent choose any set. The payoff reflects whether the proponent's set is [[CF|conflict-free]] and the attacks between the chosen sets; the value under optimal play is the strength $\mathrm{s}(a)$.
