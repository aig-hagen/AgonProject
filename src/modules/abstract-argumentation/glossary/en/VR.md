---
label: vacuous reduct
title: Vacuous Reduct Semantics
reference: T23
---

Given a base semantics $\sigma$ and a reduct semantics $\tau$, a set $E$ is a $\sigma^{\tau}$-extension of an [[AF]] $F$ iff $E \in \sigma(F)$ and the [[reduct]] $F^E$ is $\tau$-vacuous, i.e. $\tau(F^E) \subseteq \{\emptyset\}$.
