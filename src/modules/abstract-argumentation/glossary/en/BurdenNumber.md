---
label: burden number
title: Burden Number
reference: AB13
---

The Burden number of $a$ is denoted $\mathrm{Bur}(a) = \langle \mathrm{Bur}_0(a), \mathrm{Bur}_1(a), \ldots \rangle$, where $\mathrm{Bur}_0(a) = 1$ and $\mathrm{Bur}_{i}(a) = 1 + \sum_{b \in a^-} \frac{1}{\mathrm{Bur}_{i-1}(b)}$ for $i > 0$.
