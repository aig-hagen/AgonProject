A set $E \subseteq A$ is a complete [[extension]] iff $E$ is [[ADM|admissible]] and for every argument $a \in A$ [[defends|defended]] by $E$, we have $a \in E$.

## Signature

An exact characterisation of [[signature|$\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{co})$]] is open. Every $\mathbb{S} \in \mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{co})$ has $\mathbb{S} \neq \emptyset$, is com-closed and contains $\bigcap_{S \in \mathbb{S}} S$, but not every such $\mathbb{S}$ is in $\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{co})$ [@DDLW15].
