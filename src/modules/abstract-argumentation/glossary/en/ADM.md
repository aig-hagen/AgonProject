A set $E \subseteq A$ is admissible iff $E$ is [[CF|conflict-free]] and [[defends]] every $a \in E$.

## Signature

A set $\mathbb{S}$ of sets of arguments is in [[signature|$\mathsf{sig}(\mathsf{ad})$]] iff $\emptyset \in \mathbb{S}$ and $\mathbb{S}$ is conflict-sensitive [@DDLW15].
