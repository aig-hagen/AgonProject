label: social argumentation
title: Social Argumentation Ranking
---

The social argumentation ranking associates to any AF $F$ a ranking $\succeq_F^\mathrm{SA}$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A: a \succeq_F^\mathrm{SA} b$ iff $M_S(a) \geq M_S(b)$, where $M_S$ is a [[socialModel]] based on the [[simpleProductSemantics]] $S$.
