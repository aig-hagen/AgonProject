title: Simple Product Semantics
---

The simple product semantics is defined by the tuple $SP_\epsilon = \langle [0,1], \tau_\epsilon, \curlywedge, \curlyvee, \neg \rangle$, where $\tau_\epsilon = \frac{1}{1 + \epsilon}$ and $\epsilon > 0$ ensure the uniqueness, $x \curlywedge y$ is the [[productTNorm]] and $x \curlyvee y$ is the [[probabilisticSumTCoNorm]].
