title: Simple Product Semantics
reference: LM11
---

The simple product semantics is the [[saSemantics|SA-semantics]] $SP_\epsilon = \langle [0,1], \tau_\epsilon, \curlywedge, \curlyvee, \neg \rangle$ with $\epsilon > 0$, base score $\tau_\epsilon = \frac{1}{1+\epsilon}$, the [[productTNorm|product t-norm]] $\curlywedge$, the [[probabilisticSumTCoNorm|probabilistic sum t-conorm]] $\curlyvee$, and $\neg x = 1 - x$.
