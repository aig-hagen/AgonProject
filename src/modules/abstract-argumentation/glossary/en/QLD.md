label: qualified
title: Qualified Semantics
symbol: \mathsf{q}\text{-}\sigma
reference: DRT20
---

Given an [[sccDecomposability|SCC-decomposable]] [[semantics]] $\sigma$, the qualified semantics [[labelling|labels]] each [[scc|SCC]] like $\sigma$, but treats every attacker from an earlier SCC that is labelled $\mathsf{undec}$ as if it were labelled $\mathsf{out}$ (see [[qualifiedLocalFn]]).
