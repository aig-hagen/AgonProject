---
label: categorizer
title: Categorizer Ranking
---

The categorizer ranking associates to any AF $F$ a ranking $\succeq_F^\mathrm{Cat}$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A: a \succeq_F^\mathrm{Cat} b$ iff $\mathrm{Cat}_F(a) \geq \mathrm{Cat}_F(b)$, where $\mathrm{Cat}_F$ is the [[categorizerFunction]].
