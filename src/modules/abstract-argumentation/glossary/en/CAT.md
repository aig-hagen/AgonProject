---
label: categorizer
title: Categorizer Ranking
symbol: \theta^{\mathsf{Cat}}
reference: PLZL14
---

The categorizer ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ a [[rankingSemantics|ranking]] $\succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{Cat}}}_F$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A$: $a \succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{Cat}}}_F b$ iff $\mathsf{Cat}_F(a) \geq \mathsf{Cat}_F(b)$, where $\mathsf{Cat}_F$ is the [[categorizerFunction|categorizer function]].
