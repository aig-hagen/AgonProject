---
label: undisputed
title: Undisputed Sets
symbol: \mathsf{ud}
reference: T23
---

A set $E \subseteq A$ is undisputed iff $E$ is [[CF|conflict-free]] and the [[reduct]] $F^E$ has no non-empty [[ADM|admissible]] set.

## Remarks

That is, $\mathsf{ud} = \mathsf{cf}^{\mathsf{ad}}$, a [[VR|vacuous reduct semantics]].
