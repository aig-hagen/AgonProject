---
label: iterated graded defense
title: Iterated Graded Defense Ranking
---

The iterated graded defense ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ a ranking $\succeq_F^\mathrm{IGD}$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A: a \succeq_F^\mathrm{IGD} b$ iff, for every $m,n \geq 1$, whenever $b$ is [[skepticalAcceptance|skeptically accepted]] under [[mnComplete|$mn$-complete semantics]], so is $a$.
