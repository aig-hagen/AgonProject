label: iterated graded defence
title: Iterated Graded Defence Ranking
symbol: \theta^{\mathsf{IGD}}
reference: GM15
---

The iterated graded defence ranking associates to any [[AF]] $F$ a [[rankingSemantics|ranking]] $\succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{IGD}}}_F$ on $A$ such that $\forall a,b \in A$: $a \succeq^{\theta^{\mathsf{IGD}}}_F b$ iff for all $1 \le m,n \le |A|-1$, whenever $b$ is [[skepticalAcceptance|skeptically accepted]] under [[mnComplete|$mn$-complete]] semantics, so is $a$.
