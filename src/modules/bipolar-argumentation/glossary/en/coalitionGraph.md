---
label: coalition graph
title: Coalition Graph
symbol: F^{\mathsf{Coal}}_B
reference: CL10
---

The coalition graph of a [[BAF]] $B = (A, R, S)$ is the [[AF]] $F^{\mathsf{Coal}}_B = (\mathsf{Coal}(B), R^{\mathsf{Coal}}_B)$, where $\mathsf{Coal}(B)$ is the set of [[coalition|coalitions]] of $B$ and $(C_1, C_2) \in R^{\mathsf{Coal}}_B$ iff $(a, b) \in R$ for some $a \in C_1$, $b \in C_2$.
