label: coalition graph
title: Coalition Graph
---

The coalition graph $F_B^{\mathsf{Coal}}$ for some [[BAF]] B is defined as the [[AF]] $F^{\mathsf{Coal}}_B = (\mathsf{Coal}(B), R')$, where $\mathsf{Coal}(B)$ is the [[coalition|set of coalitions]] of $B$ and we have $(S_1, S_2) \in R'$ iff there are $a \in S_1$ and $b\in S_2$ with $(a,b)\in R$, for all $S_1,S_2 \in \mathsf{Coal}(B)$.
